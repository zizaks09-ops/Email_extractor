# Sample intake emails (fictional)

All names, companies and matters below are invented for testing.

## Email 1: straightforward

```
Subject: New matter – Brightline Logistics

Hi Dana,

Please open a new matter for our client Brightline Logistics. A former warehouse manager, Marcus Hale, has filed a wrongful termination claim against them. This will be handled out of our Chicago office under Illinois law. Not urgent, but please have it set up by the end of next week.

Thanks,
Robert
```

## Email 2: urgent deadline

```
Subject: URGENT – Harbor Point Hotels

Dana,

Harbor Point Hotels received an EEOC charge of discrimination from a former front desk employee, Lisa Moreno. The response is due in 10 days. Please open this in the Miami office (Florida). This is time-sensitive.

Karen
```

## Email 3: two opposing parties

```
Subject: New matter for Summit Health Partners

Hi Dana,

Summit Health Partners needs help with a wage and hour dispute. Two former nurses, James Patel and Angela Brooks, are claiming unpaid overtime. Please open it in our New York office under New York law. Normal priority.

Thanks,
Michael
```

## Email 4: casual and missing details

```
Subject: quick one

Dana – can you open something for Crestview Bank? They're dealing with a non-compete issue with a guy who left for a competitor, Tom Wheeler I think. Let's run it out of Boston. Thanks!

- Steve
```

## Email 5: deliberately messy

```
Subject: Re: Fwd: Northgate call

Dana,

Following up on my call with Northgate Manufacturing. They have an employee, Sarah Kim, who says she was retaliated against after reporting a safety issue. Her lawyer may also be naming the plant supervisor, Dave Russo, personally. Not sure yet whether this is California or Texas since she worked at both plants. Can you get this started? I'd say medium priority but it could escalate.

Paul
```
